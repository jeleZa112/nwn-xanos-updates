Implemented in GitHub: `BGR002.BAF` (ranged thief + idle FindTraps), `BGS002.BAF` (stealth scout), `BGM002.BAF` (mage control) plus pure JS `BGX_ROLE_POLICY_V03.js` decision engine and 25 deterministic tests in `BGX_ROLE_POLICY_V03.test.js`.

Current suggested roles, optional: Imoen Fighter/Thief -> ranged_thief, Montaron Fighter/Thief -> scout (hidden movement), Xzar Necromancer -> controller (sleep, blind, magic missile if memorized). Other legal kits/class combinations may be better once current level and actual spellbook confirmed.
